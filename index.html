华羽屠夫Huayu Tufu

华羽屠夫以兼顾进攻力量与挥拍速度为设计方向。刘辉在介绍中谈到，通过水标位置和平衡点调校，尝试改善传统进攻拍与挥速拍之间的取舍。黑粉涂装搭配白色握把，形成鲜明的外观识别。Huayu Tufu is designed to balance attacking power with swing speed. In his introduction, Liu Hui explains how decal placement and balance-point tuning aim to address the trade-off between power-focused and speed-focused rackets. A black-and-pink finish and white grip give it a distinctive appearance.

这支球拍保留了一定的上手门槛。影片除了介绍设计思路，还展示了反手、推挑等实际击球，并讨论挥拍与发力感受。可以结合完整演示，了解它与自己技术习惯的契合程度。The racket has a learning curve. Alongside the design explanation, the film includes backhand and push-and-lift demonstrations and a discussion of swing and hitting feel. Watch the full demonstration to consider how it may suit your technique.

紫电青霜Zidian Qingshuang

紫电青霜以深色拍身配合紫色与金色细节，白色握把形成鲜明对比。拍框采用华羽独立设计与自主开模，设计重点是击球稳定性；拍框、锥盖与中杆的涂装也在影片中逐一展示。Zidian Qingshuang pairs a dark body with purple and gold detailing and a contrasting white grip. Huayu describes an independently designed frame made with its own mould, with shot stability as a design priority. The film shows the finish across the frame, cone and shaft.

据品牌介绍，双结构中杆将弯折点上移，着重于加快回弹；双层内发泡工艺则用于改善击球时的减震表现。想进一步了解这些设计如何体现在使用中，可以观看刘辉的完整讲解。According to the brand, the dual-structure shaft moves the bending point upward to promote a quicker rebound, while dual-layer internal foaming is intended to dampen vibration at impact. Watch Liu Hui’s introduction for more on the thinking behind these features.

华羽球服Huayu teamwear

华羽球服首发提供紫、黑、红、蓝、白五种配色，发布视频由五位成员穿着不同颜色同场展示。短袖搭配撞色领口、肩部线条与袖口，胸前以华羽标识和 Badminton 字样呈现羽毛球主题。Huayu’s launch collection offers five colours: purple, black, red, blue and white. Five members wear the different colours together in the launch film. The short-sleeve design features a contrasting neckline, shoulder lines and cuffs, with the Huayu emblem and Badminton lettering on the chest.

展厅展示的是黑色款正面：深色衣身叠加灰色图案，以白色领边、肩线和袖边勾勒轮廓，袖部带有 HUAYU 标识。可通过首发影片比较五款配色的实穿效果，再向华羽咨询尺码和购买信息。The gallery presents the front of the black version: a dark body with grey graphics, outlined by white trim at the neck, shoulders and cuffs, plus HUAYU branding on the sleeve. Compare all five colours in the launch film, then contact Huayu for sizing and purchasing information.
